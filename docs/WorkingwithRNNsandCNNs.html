Working with RNNs and CNNs

Predicting the future is something we do all the time, whether we are finishing someone else’s sentence during a conversation or anticipating the smell of coffee when we enter a cafe.

In lieu of this instruction, let’s discuss RNNRecurrent Neural Networksa type of ANN designed to process sequential data, such as text, speech, or time series, by passing information through loops from one step to the nexts:

RNNRecurrent Neural Networksa type of ANN designed to process sequential data, such as text, speech, or time series, by passing information through loops from one step to the nexts can analyze time series data.

For example, the number of daily active users on our shopping website, the hourly temperature in Innsbruck, our homes daily power consumption, the trajectories of nearby cars, and much more.

Once an RNNRecurrent Neural Networksa type of ANN designed to process sequential data, such as text, speech, or time series, by passing information through loops from one step to the next learns past patterns in the data, it is able to use its knowledge to forecast the future, assuming, of course, that past patterns still hold in the future. More generally, RNNRecurrent Neural Networksa type of ANN designed to process sequential data, such as text, speech, or time series, by passing information through loops from one step to the nexts can work on sequences of arbitrary lengths, rather than on fixed-sized inputs. [186] For example, they can take sentences, documents, or audio samples as input, which makes them extremely useful for NLPNatural Language ProcessingNatural Language Processing applications such as automatic translation or speech-to-text.
