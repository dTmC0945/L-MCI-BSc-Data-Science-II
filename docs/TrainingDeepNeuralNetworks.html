Chapter 3
Training Deep Neural Networks

In Understanding PyTorch we’ve built, trained, and fine-tuned several ANNArtificial Neural NetworksArtificial Neural Networkss using PyTorch. But they were shallow nets with just a few hidden layers.

What if we need to tackle a complex problem, such as detecting hundreds of types of objects in high-resolution images?

We may need to train a much deeper ANNArtificial Neural NetworksArtificial Neural Networks, perhaps with dozens or even hundreds of layers, each containing hundreds of neurons, linked by hundreds of thousands of connections. Training a DNNDeep Neural NetworksDeep Neural Networks isn’t a walk in the park. Here are some of the problems we could run into:

  • Gradients growing smaller or larger when flowing backward through the DNNDeep Neural NetworksDeep Neural Networks during training. [117] Both of these problems make lower layers very hard to train.

  • Not having enough training data for such a large network, or the data being too costly to label.

  • Training taking impractical amount of time.

  • A model with millions of parameters risks severely overfitting the training set, especially if there are not enough training instances or if they are too noisy.
